Resort shortlist

Pick one base for up to four ski days. Interlaken Ost is a rail base for the Jungfrau options.

Sörenberg · Village slopes Start near the village and ask the ski school which lift suits your level. Take the train to Schüpfheim and continue by bus. Sörenberg lift prices
Engelberg · Brunni / Klostermatte Take the train via Lucerne to Engelberg, then the local bus to Brunni. Klostermatte has practice lifts and magic carpets beside the valley station. Klostermatte terrain and access
Grindelwald · Bodmi Arena Start here for a first lesson or a day on the magic carpet. Take the train from Interlaken Ost to Grindelwald and continue by local bus to Bodmi. Bodmi passes and lessons
Wengen · Grindelwald–Wengen ski area Take the train via Lauterbrunnen to car-free Wengen. Once you can link turns, look for blue runs around Kleine Scheidegg and Männlichen on the piste map. Terrain and ski passes
Mürren · Allmendhubel Start on the practice terrain at Allmendhubel. From Interlaken Ost, take the train to Lauterbrunnen, then continue to Mürren and ride the Allmendhubel funicular. Mürren winter map and lifts
Zermatt · Sunnegga Take the train via Visp to Zermatt, then ride the Sunnegga funicular. The beginner pass covers Wolli’s Park and the Sunnegga–Blauherd practice area. Sunnegga beginner pass

Ways to save

Start with local lift accessBuy a practice-area ticket or a short lift pass for the first session. Compare the full-area pass when you know where you want to ski next.
Check the lesson packageAsk the ski school whether the lesson includes lift access or rentals before you book them separately.
Rent for the days you skiCompare the four-day prices with shorter rentals if you’re leaving a day open for sightseeing or weather.
